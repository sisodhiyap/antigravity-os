## 2. The 15-Phase Creation Workflow

When you click **Generate Deck**, Hermes autonomously coordinates 15 deterministic steps:
1. **UNDERSTANDING**: Intent decomposition & prompt extraction.
2. **PLANNING**: Slide outline & story structure planning.
3. **RESEARCH**: Fact retrieval & domain knowledge gathering.
4. **TRUST**: Factuality grounding against Trust Fabric.
5. **STORY**: Story Graph construction & emotional tension arc.
6. **CREATIVE DIRECTION**: Core thesis, visual metaphor, and rhythm synthesis.
7. **DESIGN**: Design tokens and 9 aesthetic directions.
8. **MEDIA**: ComfyUI DirectML media bible & vector fallback.
9. **BUILD**: Slide assembly across 22 composition layouts.
10. **AUDIT**: Quality, contrast, and hierarchy scoring.
11. **AUTO-REPAIR**: Localized defect correction.
12. **DECK HEALTH**: Holistic pacing & repetition radar audit.
13. **SECURITY**: Adversarial payload sanitization & AST verification.
14. **EXPORT**: OpenXML binary PPTX, PDF, and signed JSON packaging.
15. **BASKET**: Archival in `workspaces/presentx-vault/`.

## 3. Inline Slide Editor & AI Assistant

### Direct Canvas Editing
Click on any headline, subtitle, metric card, bullet point, or quote to edit text directly.
- **Truth Invalidation**: Editing verified metrics or factual assertions immediately flags the claim for re-verification (`VERIFIED` ➔ `UNVERIFIED`).

### AI Contextual Assistant Actions
Select any slide and run:
- **REWRITE**: Elevate tone to high-impact strategic register.
- **SHORTEN**: Condense text to executive glanceable takeaways.
- **EXPAND**: Add supporting technical proof and metrics.
- **MAKE EXECUTIVE**: Focus on margin expansion and business upside.
- **CHANGE LAYOUT**: Swap between 22 layout families.
- **ADD SPEAKER NOTES**: Generate teleprompter presenter guidance.
- **AUTO REPAIR**: Execute localized hierarchy and contrast fixes.

---

## 4. Presenter Mode & Teleprompter

Press **Present** in the top toolbar or press `P`:
- **Dual Display Teleprompter**: Fullscreen presentation with live elapsed timer.
- **Presenter Notes**: View speaker notes synchronized with slide progress (toggle with `N`).
- **Interactive Laser Pointer**: Hover with red virtual laser (toggle with `L`).
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Arrow Right / Space for next slide, Arrow Left for previous, Escape to return to editor.

---

## 5. Multi-Format Export Factory

- **PowerPoint (.PPTX)**: Genuine OpenXML ZIP archive containing widescreen 16:9 slides, theme palettes, and notes.
- **Interactive Web Deck (.HTML)**: Self-contained responsive HTML with keyboard navigation and WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- **Evidence Bundle (.JSON)**: Cryptographically signed manifest with fact claim provenance hashes.
