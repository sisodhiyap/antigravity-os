Welcome to **PresentX Studio**, the AI presentation design platform powered by **Antigravity OS V7**.

## 1. Creating Your First Presentation

### Step 1: Enter Your Idea
Navigate to `/presentx` on your desktop, tablet, or mobile device. Enter your core topic in the idea bar:
> *"Create a 10-slide investor pitch deck for an autonomous creative studio platform with $3M ARR."*

Or click **"New Presentation"** to launch the step-by-step wizard, allowing you to choose:
- Objective & Title
- Presentation Type (Pitch Deck, Investor Deck, Case Study, Educational, Report, Storytelling)
- Slide Count (6, 8, 10, 12 slides)
- Visual Style & Direction (Editorial, Minimal, Corporate, Tech, Futuristic, Creative, Luxury, Data-Driven, Academic)

### Step 2: Instant Autonomous Synthesis
Click **"Generate Deck"**. Within seconds, Hermes and the V7 Trust Fabric will:
1. Synthesize a structured Presentation Brief.
2. Ground all factual assertions and claims.
3. Formulate an intentional narrative Story Graph.
4. Design structured slide layouts with real SVG charts and diagrams.
5. Create a ComfyUI Media Bible for visual consistency.

## 2. Using the Slide Editor (`/presentx/editor/[id]`)

The professional slide editor is organized into three dynamic zones:

- **Left Slide Navigator**: Reorder slides with drag-and-drop, add new slides, duplicate existing slides, or delete slides.
- **Center WYSIWYG Canvas**: Click directly on headings, subheadings, or bullet points to edit inline in real time.
- **Right AI & Inspector Panel**:
  - **AI Assist**: One-click actions to *Rewrite Clearer*, *Make Executive*, *Make Persuasive*, *Condense*, *Expand*, *Attach Fact Grounding*, or *Generate ComfyUI Visual*.
  - **Layout**: Switch instantly between Hero, Two-Column, Metrics Grid, Chart View, Process Flow, Quote, and Conclusion layouts.
  - **Style**: Change visual directions on the fly across 9 curated themes.
  - **Audit**: View real-time WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility and factual grounding scores.

---

## 3. Presenting in Distraction-Free Mode (`/presentx/present/[id]`)

Click **"Present"** on the top toolbar to enter distraction-free presentation mode:
- **Left / Right / Spacebar**: Navigate slides.
- **F**: Toggle fullscreen.
- **N**: Toggle presenter teleprompter speaker notes.
- **L**: Toggle virtual laser pointer.
- **Escape**: Exit back to editor.

---

## 4. Exporting Your Presentation

Click **"Export"** to choose your desired format:
1. **PowerPoint (.PPTX)**: Fully editable slide shapes, text boxes, and speaker notes.
2. **Interactive Web Deck (.HTML)**: Self-contained single file that opens in any browser.
3. **Signed Evidence Bundle (.JSON)**: Complete cryptographic project state and evidence ledger.
