# Antigravity OS V7.0 — Fact-Based Trust Fabric Guide

## 1. Factuality Principles
The Trust Fabric enforces evidence-grounded intelligence:
- **Zero-Hallucination Promotion Barrier**: Claims cannot transition to `VERIFIED` without verifiable execution evidence (`E4` sandbox or `E5` physical host execution).
- **Temporal Freshness**: Claims automatically expire and downgrade to `STALE` when past their validity window.
- **Contradiction Arbitration**: When sources conflict, a `ContradictionSet` is constructed, downgrading confidence until empirical proof resolves the dispute.
