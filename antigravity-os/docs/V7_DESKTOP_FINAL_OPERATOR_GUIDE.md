# ANTIGRAVITY OS V7 — NATIVE DESKTOP OPERATOR GUIDE

## 1. Starting Antigravity OS Desktop
1. Double-click the **Antigravity OS** desktop icon or launch from the **Windows Start Menu**.
2. The **Startup Diagnostic Screen** automatically checks CPU, RAM, GPU, VRAM, Ollama, ComfyUI, Hermes, Trust Fabric, and Evidence Ledger.
3. Click **ENTER COMMAND CENTER** to enter the workspace.

---

## 2. "Create from Idea" Presentation Generation
1. In the Command Center, enter your prompt in the **Create from Idea** input box (e.g. *"10-slide presentation about the future of AI in creative agencies"*).
2. Click **CREATE DECK**.
3. Hermes and the Source Reality Gate autonomously extract verified claims, build the story graph, structure layouts, and seal the deck with the Truth Firewall.
4. Edit, present fullscreen, or export to PPTX/HTML/JSON.

---

## 3. Local Service Control & Emergency Stop
- **Restarting Services**: Click **Restart** on any service tile in the Local Supervisor section.
- **Viewing Logs**: Click **View Logs** to stream isolated log outputs.
- **Emergency Stop**: Click the red **EMERGENCY STOP** button in the top navigation bar to halt non-essential AI services immediately.
