# ANTIGRAVITY OS V7 — DESKTOP OPERATOR GUIDE

## 1. Launching Antigravity OS Desktop
1. Double-click the **Antigravity OS** desktop icon or launch from the **Windows Start Menu**.
2. On startup, the **Hardware Reality Detector** automatically inspects CPU, RAM, GPU, VRAM, and local AI engines.
3. The **Command Center** appears with all 6 background services supervised and active.

---

## 2. "Create from Idea" Presentation Creation
1. In the Command Center, locate the **Create from Idea** input banner.
2. Enter your topic or prompt (e.g. *"12-slide executive presentation on sovereign AI infrastructure"*).
3. Click **Create Deck**.
4. The system executes Hermes $\to$ Source Reality Gate $\to$ PresentX Studio $\to$ Truth Firewall.
5. Edit, inspect claim provenance, present fullscreen, or export to PPTX/HTML/JSON.

---

## 3. Local Service Control & Emergency Stop
- **Restarting Services**: Click **Restart** on any service tile in the Command Center.
- **Viewing Logs**: Click **View Logs** to stream isolated log outputs.
- **Emergency Stop**: Click the **EMERGENCY STOP** button in the top navigation bar to halt non-essential AI services immediately.
