## 2. Output Governance & Firewalls

1. **Numerical Claim Firewall**:
   - Enforces validation of `VALUE`, `UNIT`, `TIME PERIOD`, `SCOPE`, `SOURCE`, `SOURCE LOCATION`, `EVIDENCE`, and `PROVENANCE`.
   - Insufficient evidence causes automatic conversion to `ILLUSTRATIVE_DATA` or `UNVERIFIED`.
2. **Chart Firewall**:
   - Charts explicitly declare `REAL_DATA`, `USER_PROVIDED_DATA`, `ILLUSTRATIVE_DATA`, or `GENERATED_DATA`.
   - Embeds `dataset_hash`, `source_hash`, `units`, and `time_range`.
3. **Change Detection & Invalidation**:
   - Post-verification edits to slide text, numbers, or notes immediately invalidate prior verification (`VERIFIED` $\to$ `UNVERIFIED`).
4. **Visual Factuality**:
   - AI-generated images, diagrams, and timelines receive explicit `GENERATED_VISUAL` metadata so they cannot be misconstrued as empirical graphs.
5. **Pre-Export Scan & Cryptographic Manifest**:
   - Blocks exports containing ungrounded claims or requires explicit Owner Override with immutable audit logging.
   - Seals every export with a signed `ExportManifest` (`presentationHash`, `sourceHashes`, `claimHashes`, `evidenceHashes`, `datasetHashes`, `mediaHashes`, `signature`).
